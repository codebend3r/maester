# Raven, the Mac app

Raven is raven as a Mac app, for Apple Silicon: the same pages, sign-in and library as the browser, but every video plays through mpv rather than the browser, so MKV, HEVC, Dolby audio, DTS and TrueHD all play as they are, with no conversion. It can also keep copies of titles on the Mac to watch without the server.

1. On the repo's Releases page (github.com/codebend3r/maester/releases), pick the newest entry with three numbers, such as `v0.6.0`, and download `raven-player-<version>-arm64.dmg`.
2. Open the dmg and drag Raven to Applications.
3. Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app: open **System Settings, Privacy & Security**, choose **Open Anyway** beside Raven, and confirm with an administrator's password. You do this once.
4. Sign in with your raven email and password, as on the web.

**Download** sits beside Play on every movie, episode, season and show page; the **Downloads** page in the side menu lists them, and plays them with no server at all.
