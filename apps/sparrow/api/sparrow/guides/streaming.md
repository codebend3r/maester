## Picture and sound in raven

**Quality.** The player's Playback menu has a **Quality** list: **Play original quality**, then **Convert to ...** for each size below the file (1080p at 20 Mb/s down to 328p at 0.7 Mb/s). Converting holds the stream to that size's bitrate, so pick a smaller one on slow wifi or mobile data, and the original at home.

**Too slow for the file.** When a repackaged stream arrives slower than it plays, the player steps down to a converted one from the same spot instead of stalling, and says what the connection carried. A 4K HDR remux runs 70 to 80 Mb/s, more than wifi a room or two from the router often carries.

**Audio and subtitles.** The settings button (or `c` for subtitles) lists every audio and subtitle track. Picking an audio track other than the file's default plays through a repackaged stream. In a browser, picture subtitles (PGS, VobSub) are drawn into the picture, which means converting the video; pick a text track or turn them off if that stutters.
