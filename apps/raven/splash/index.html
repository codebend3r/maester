Free to run on your own machine. Read the source

  • Kept on your own disk

    Your films, shows and albums stay in the folders where they already live. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • One library, two apps

    Raven plays your video. Wren plays the music on the same Raven server, albums and playlists included.

  • Nobody else's servers

    Accounts you approve and a PIN for private libraries. Nothing leaves your network unless you say so.
