Your videos, played from your own disk.

Point raven at the folders you keep your videos in. It indexes them, makes a thumbnail for each, and plays them straight from the file, converting only what the player cannot play as it is.

For Macs with Apple silicon and Windows PCs. Every version

Plays what you already have
Films, shows, home video, anything in a folder. Titles and posters come from TMDB, matched to your files.
Direct from disk
A file the player can handle is streamed as it is. Only what it cannot play is remuxed or transcoded, on the fly, on your own machine.
Yours alone
Accounts you approve, a PIN for the libraries you keep private, and a history of what each viewer watched. Nothing leaves your network unless you say so.
On every screen
The web app in any browser, the Raven app on a Mac or a Windows PC with its own player, and the device app for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Android TV.

raven is open source. The code, the roadmap and every release are on GitHub.
