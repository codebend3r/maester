Your music, played from your own disk.

Wren plays the albums and playlists on your Raven server. It streams each track as the file it is, keeps what you download for when you are offline, and goes on playing with the screen locked.

Start with Raven

For iPhone, iPad and Android phones. Wren is still being built and has no release yet; it signs in with your Raven account.

  • The file itself

    A track streams as the file on your disk, lossless when it is. On a mobile network you can choose a smaller stream, and only then is it converted.

  • Yours offline

    Download an album or a playlist to the phone, at the quality you pick, and it plays with no network at all.

  • Plays on

    Line up what comes next, reorder the queue, and keep listening from the lock screen while the phone is in your pocket.
