# Fire TV

1. From the Fire TV home screen, search for **Plex** (or find it in the **Appstore**) and download it.
2. Open Plex and choose **Sign In**. It shows a code: go to **plex.tv/link** on your phone or computer, sign in with your Plex account, and enter the code.
3. The server's libraries show up once you've accepted the invite; pin the ones you use to Home from the sidebar.

A Fire TV Stick 4K or Fire TV Cube plays most 4K, HDR and HEVC files directly. An older Fire TV Stick is 1080p: pick the 1080p version of a title. Dolby Vision profile 7 copies (Blu-ray remuxes) don't play right on a Fire TV (a purple or green picture, or nothing); ask me for another version.
