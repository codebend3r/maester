# Android TV, Google TV and Nvidia Shield

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on the TV or box, search for **Plex**, and install it.
2. Open Plex and choose **Sign In**. It shows a code: go to **plex.tv/link** on your phone or computer, sign in with your Plex account, and enter the code.
3. The server's libraries show up once you've accepted the invite; pin the ones you use to Home from the sidebar.

An **Nvidia Shield** plays nearly everything directly, Dolby Vision profile 7 remuxes included, and with **audio passthrough** on it sends TrueHD and DTS to a receiver as they are. Other Android TV and Google TV devices vary: when a 4K file stutters, pick the 1080p version, and for a Dolby Vision profile 7 remux (a purple or green picture, or nothing), ask me for another version.
