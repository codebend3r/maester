# Apple TV

1. Open the **App Store** on the Apple TV, search for **Plex**, and install it.
2. Open Plex and choose **Sign In**. It shows a code: go to **plex.tv/link** on your phone or computer, sign in with your Plex account, and enter the code.
3. The server's libraries show up once you've accepted the invite. If they don't, open the sidebar and look under **More**, or pin them to Home from there.
4. In the Apple TV's own **Settings → Video and Audio → Match Content**, turn on **Match Dynamic Range** and **Match Frame Rate**, so HDR and 24 fps films look as they should.

An Apple TV 4K plays 4K, HDR and HEVC files directly. TrueHD and DTS soundtracks are converted by the server on Apple TV; that's normal.
